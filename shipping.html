Shipping Policy

Seva Care is a service booking platform. We do not ship physical goods. All services such as massage, cleaning, loading, labour work, etc. are performed in person by workers at the customer location.

Key Points

  • No physical product delivery or shipping is involved in our services.
  • All bookings are for in-person service only.
  • If any physical item is needed during service, it is handled directly between customer and worker.
