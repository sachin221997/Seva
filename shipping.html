Shipping Policy

Seva Care is a service booking platform. We do not ship physical goods. All services such as massage, cleaning, loading, labour work, etc., are performed in person by workers at the customer's location.

Key Points

  • No physical product delivery or shipping is involved in our platform.
  • All bookings are for in-person services only.
  • Any physical item required during service is handled directly between customer and worker.
