How Can We Help You?

Welcome to the Seva App Help Center. Here you will find answers to common questions and support for using Seva smoothly.

How to Post a Job (Customer)

  • Go to the Post Need page
  • Fill details (work type, description, amount, location)
  • Submit → Your job will appear to workers in your city
  • Workers will contact you & complete the task

How Workers Earn Money

  • Open the Worker Dashboard
  • See jobs posted in your city
  • Accept a job → Contact customer
  • Complete work → Earn money
  • Pay ₹50 platform fee before job completion

Platform Fee (Important)

Seva App charges a ₹50 platform fee per job from workers. This helps keep the app running, secure, and free for customers.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Job accept nahi ho raha?

Make sure your location & city match the job’s location.

2. Customer reply nahi kar raha?

Wait for some time. Many customers take time to respond.

3. Payment kaise milega?

Customer directly aapko amount dega after work completion.

4. Platform fee kab dena hota hai?

Work accept hone ke baad worker ₹50 admin ko pay karega.
