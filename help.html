Help & Support

Need help? Don’t worry! This page will guide you on how to use the app, post needs, find work, and get support.

How to Post a Need

  • Open Need Page
  • Fill the title, description, location
  • (Optional) Add budget & image
  • Click on Post Need
  • Your need will show in the “Posted Needs” list

People nearby can see your need and help you.

How to Find Work

If you have skills, you can earn daily or per hour by helping people.

  • Open Jobs Page
  • Search needs posted by users
  • Contact them and provide the service
  • Earn money directly from the customer

You can work anytime — part-time or full-time.

Frequently Asked Questions

✔️ Is this app free?

Yes, 100% free to use.

✔️ How do I earn?

You earn by helping people with the skills you have.

✔️ Is my data safe?

Yes, everything is stored safely on your device or via your account.

✔️ Do I need any special qualification?

No. Anyone with basic skills can start earning.
