About Seva Care App

About Seva Care App

Seva Care App is a simple and powerful platform where anyone with skills can work, help others, and earn money daily or per hour.

Our mission is to make work accessible for everyone — students, workers, homemakers, skilled professionals, and anyone who wants to earn through their talent.

How Seva Care App Works

For People Who Need Help:

  • Post your need (cleaning, tuition, delivery, repair, cooking, dating, Helping etc.)
  • Nearby workers can view your job
  • They contact you & complete the work

For People Who Want to Earn:

  • Go to the Jobs page
  • Find available work near you
  • Use your skill → help others → earn money

Why We Created This App

India me har person ke paas koi na koi skill hoti hai — par sabko mauka nahi milta.

Seva Care App ka goal hai:

  • Every skill should have value
  • Everyone should get work easily
  • People should earn daily without any struggle
  • Community help & trust build ho

Who Can Use This App?

  • Students
  • Skilled workers
  • Home makers
  • Tutors, electricians, plumbers
  • Delivery helpers
  • Anyone who wants to earn or get help
